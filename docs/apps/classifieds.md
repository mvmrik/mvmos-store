# Classifieds

Classifieds lets you view a listing over its conversation without closing the chat.

## Photos in messages

Next to the message box, "Attach a photo" adds one JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB, with or without text, so people can exchange pictures of an item. The sender can also choose that the photo disappears 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute or 5 minutes after the recipient opens it. The recipient taps to open it, sees a countdown, and when the time ends the file is deleted from the server. The sender sees whether it was opened, and the administrator can see such photos in message moderation until they expire. A browser cannot prevent screenshots.

## Message moderation

The Messages tab of the desktop Moderation panel opens on conversations with messages that are new to the administrator, without system notices. Filters show seen conversations or everything and can include system notices. "Mark all as seen" moves only the administrator's own marker; the people in the conversations are not affected. A count of new messages appears on the Moderation button and the Messages tab.
