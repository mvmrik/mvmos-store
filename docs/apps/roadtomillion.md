# The Road to Millions

The Road to Millions 1.1.0 is a quiz game for Game Hub. Twenty questions grow harder from the first to the last, which is worth 1,000,000. Play alone, or share the same questions with up to six players.

## Open the game

Install The Road to Millions from the Games category. Its desktop launcher opens the Game Hub play page and offers to install Game Hub if it is missing. Sign in with an Apps Hub profile. Play alone or create a multiplayer room and invite other players; people can use different computers, phones and tablets in a web browser.

In multiplayer, the host chooses a turn time of 20, 30, 45, 60 or 90 seconds. Solo play has no turn clock.

## Questions and sums

Each question has four answers, shown in a different order every time. A correct answer climbs one step of the ladder. The safe sums wait after questions 5, 10 and 15, which are 1,000, 7,500 and 50,000. A wrong answer drops you to the last safe sum you passed, and walking away at any moment keeps the sum you have reached. Playing alone, you can continue past the million with further questions.

## Lifelines

Before the first question, choose three of ten specialists (mathematics, history, medicine, astronomy, law, geography, engineering, literature, arts and sport) and three of nine countries other than your own. During the game, each of the four lifelines can be used once:

• Remove two wrong answers.
• Ask one of your specialists, who knows some subjects better than others.
• Poll a country, whose people know some questions better than others, depending on the question and its origin.
• Use the compass to check one answer without risk. It tells you whether that answer is right, and you still choose your own answer; a wrong answer is removed.

In multiplayer, everyone still in the game answers the same question, and the other players see who used a lifeline but not what it said.

## Scores and statistics

Your score is your winnings plus percentage bonuses: a bonus for using few or no lifelines over a game of at least ten questions, a bonus for being right when the advice you were given was wrong, and a bonus for three or more correct answers in a row without any lifeline. The final panel shows the amounts and the bonus breakdown. Game Hub keeps finished sessions, rankings and the declared statistics: the amount won, the bonus percentage, questions answered, lifelines used and the size of the question bank.

## The question bank

The questions come with the app, in all nine mvmOS languages, and the server decides which ones appear and how hard they are. The browsers only receive what is needed for the question on screen. In the desktop app, the owner can open the question manager: browse and filter the bank, edit a question and its answers in every language, import questions from a text file, and add new ones. When mvmAI is set up and its App API is enabled, the manager can fill in a draft, suggest questions, finish a half-written one and review a question's answer. New questions that the owner adds are kept in the installation's own data and are not overwritten when the app is updated.

## Availability

Requires mvmOS Core 1.16.0 or later and Game Hub. The desktop app is a launcher; play takes place on the Game Hub web page. mvmAI is optional and only used by the question manager; using its sessions needs mvmAI 0.24.0. The app supplies its translations in all nine mvmOS languages and has no Premium module.

## Question editing in 1.1.0

The AI editor accepts an optional instruction for an individual question and checks that its wording includes enough context to stand alone in every language. Import can repair malformed question JSON before review; no repaired questions are added until the administrator accepts them. Editing pasted import text clears the previous review.

The bundled question bank is expanded in all nine languages. In multiplayer, questions with difficulty 1 are used only for the first question.
