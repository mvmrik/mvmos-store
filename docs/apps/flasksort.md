# Flask Sort

Flask Sort is a liquid sorting puzzle for one to six players. Open its launcher in mvmOS and choose Play to reach the public Game Hub page. Game Hub is required; the launcher offers to install it when it is missing. Players use their Apps Hub accounts and can join from computers, phones or tablets in a web browser. The 3D flasks need WebGL.

## How to play

Tap a flask to lift it, then tap another flask to pour into it. The top layer of colour pours as far as the other flask has room. It may go onto the same colour or onto a different one, so you can park a dose temporarily to free space; a level is never stuck, and undo is always there. A level is cleared when every flask is empty or full of a single colour; full flasks are sealed with a lid.

You do not have to wait for a pour to end: choose the next flask and pour while the current one is still moving, and it starts as soon as the one before is done. A single dose pours quickly and several at once take longer.

The buttons at the bottom undo the last pour, restart the level and give a hint; in a multiplayer game a fourth button gives up. A hint lifts the flask to pour from and lights the rim of the flask to pour into, and costs the points of one flask of the level, even if your points go below zero; the first one in a game asks you to confirm. The sound button mutes or restores sounds on this device.

## Levels

A game has 100 levels, and each one is harder than the last. The first has two colours and small flasks of three doses. As you climb, new colours arrive and the flasks gradually hold more doses, until the last levels have twenty colours in flasks of eight doses. The server deals every level and checks that it can be solved. The colours are chosen so that no two look alike.

All the flasks of a level are always on the screen. On a phone they stand in up to three rows on shelves, on a wide screen in fewer rows, and the picture is scaled to fit.

## Points

Every dose is worth 10 points, so a flask is worth its doses: 40 points for a flask of 4 doses, 80 for 8. Everybody who clears a level gets the same base points, which are all the doses of the level, and this is the largest part.

Two bonuses come on top. The speed bonus is one point for every second left of the time the level allows, which is as many seconds a move as there are doses in a flask (4 seconds for 4 doses, 8 for 8), for the moves of the level's par; a bar under the level shows what is left. The par bonus works like golf and is paid in flasks: par is the number of colour layers in the level as dealt (neighbouring doses of one colour are one layer, and the bottom layer of each flask counts too), the same rule on every level and for every player. Finishing in par moves earns the price of one flask of the level, one move under par two flasks, and two or more moves under par three flasks, which is the most. Being over par costs nothing. Every pour counts as a move, including undone ones.

A hint costs the points of one flask of the level (10 points a dose), even if that takes your points below zero.

When you clear a level the result stays on screen, and the next level's clock only starts when you choose Next level.

## Playing alone

A solo game runs from level 1 to level 100. It ends when you clear level 100, or when you choose Finish game on the screen after any level. Only then is your score recorded in Game Hub, and it is your total points for the game. The next game starts again from level 1, and your best finished game is your record. Game Hub's Save & exit keeps the exact position without recording an unfinished session; when you start again, Game Hub asks whether to continue the saved game or start a new one.

## Playing with others

The host chooses how many levels to play, up to 100. Everyone plays the same levels at their own pace, up to that level, and scores exactly as when playing alone. A player who clears the last level waits for the others; a player can also give up and keeps the points earned so far. When everybody is done, the most total points win, so being faster only earns a speed bonus, not the win. A tie goes to whoever finished first. Everyone's points are recorded.

## Statistics

Game Hub records the level reached, levels cleared, levels solved within par, moves, undos, hints used and the most colours met, with a rating for multiplayer.

The records never take space from the board while you play. After a clear, the breakdown compares the level score with your personal best and the overall best for that numbered level. This score includes the level bonuses and subtracts hints paid for on that level; the total run score remains separate. Completed level records survive Save & exit. Tracking starts with this feature because older runs did not preserve individual level scores. A continued older save with hints starts tracking records from its next fresh level.
