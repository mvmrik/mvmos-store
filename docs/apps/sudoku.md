# Sudoku

Sudoku is a number puzzle for one to six players. Open its launcher in mvmOS and choose Play to reach the public Game Hub page. Game Hub 1.18.0 or newer is required; the launcher offers to install it when it is missing. Players use their Apps Hub accounts and can join from computers, phones or tablets in a web browser.

## How to play

Fill the grid so that every row, column and 3×3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9. Choose a difficulty: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert or Master. Every puzzle is made for the game and has exactly one solution; the easier levels can be solved by logic alone, while Expert and Master make you look further ahead. The server keeps the solution and checks every number.

Play with the mouse or keyboard on a computer, or with your finger on a phone. The board highlights the row, column, box and every matching number, the number pad shows how many of each are left, and a short ripple marks a completed row, column or box. Pencil notes clear themselves as numbers are placed. Buttons undo, erase and give a hint. On a keyboard use the number keys, the arrows, N for notes and H for a hint.

## Points

There is no time limit and time never ends the game, but the clock slowly costs points, one every 10 seconds on Easy up to one every 30 seconds on Master, and the time is kept for your statistics. Every correct number scores points, from 10 on Easy up to 18 on Master. A mistake costs twice a cell's points, so guessing does not pay. A hint fills in one cell and earns nothing for it; it costs the points of two cells plus a few more for every cell already filled in, so it is dearer the further the puzzle has gone, and it can take your points below zero. Solving the puzzle with no mistakes and no hints adds a bonus.

## Playing alone

A solo game is one puzzle, and your score is recorded when you solve it. Game Hub's Save & exit keeps the exact position, with your notes, and records nothing; when you start again, Game Hub asks whether to continue the saved game or start a new one.

## Playing with others

The host chooses the level and everybody solves the same puzzle at their own pace. When everybody has finished, the player with the most points wins. A player who has finished can watch the other boards fill in, or leave: the result is already recorded and counts when the game ends.

## Statistics

Game Hub records points, level, puzzles solved, solving time, mistakes and hints, with a rating for multiplayer.

The score display and completed puzzle screen compare your current score with your personal best and the overall best for the selected difficulty. Only successfully completed puzzles set these records, including multiplayer puzzles while the other players are still playing. Tracking starts with this feature; historical difficulty records are not reconstructed from incomplete session data. The final game screen also shows the Game Hub top 10 and your ranking position.
