# Deal or Not

Deal or Not 1.0.0 is a Game Hub game for one to six players. Everyone shares a board of 36 boxes, with different amounts up to one million.

## Open the game

Install Deal or Not from the Games category. Its desktop launcher opens the Game Hub play page and offers to install Game Hub if it is missing. Sign in with an Apps Hub profile. Play alone or create a multiplayer room and invite other players; people can use different computers, phones and tablets in a web browser.

In multiplayer, the host chooses a turn time of 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds. Solo play has no turn clock.

## Boxes and banker calls

Each player draws a random numbered box. Players then take turns opening boxes left on the table. The server keeps every amount hidden until its box is opened; a revealed amount is removed from the possibilities for everyone.

After each round, the banker calls all players who are still playing, with a private offer of money or a swap. Accept or refuse your offer. When accepting a swap, choose an available box. Everyone's decisions are revealed together when the answers are in. Taking money ends your own play, while you can watch the others finish. Refusing every offer leaves you with the amount in your final box.

## Scores and saves

Your score combines winnings with percentage bonuses for your decisions. The final panel shows the amounts and bonus breakdown. Game Hub keeps finished sessions, rankings and the declared statistics: winnings, bonus percentage, refused offers and board size.

Use Save & exit to put down a solo run and continue it later through Game Hub. Saving keeps an unfinished game rather than recording a finished score. Multiplayer rooms cannot be saved by one player.

## Availability

Requires mvmOS Core 1.16.0 or later and Game Hub. The desktop app is a launcher; play takes place on the Game Hub web page. The app supplies its translations in all nine mvmOS languages and has no Premium module.
