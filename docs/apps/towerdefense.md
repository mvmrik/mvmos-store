# Tower Defense

Tower Defense is a solo game played through the public Game Hub page. The desktop window opens the game and offers to install Game Hub when needed. Players sign in with their Apps Hub profile; completed runs appear in their Game Hub history and rankings.

## Defend and upgrade

The tower fights automatically during each wave. Between waves, spend coins on weapons, energy, repairs, tower upgrades and battlefield defences. Upgrade buttons have two rows: what they improve, then their price and the extra energy they will draw.

Bought items show only their name and current values. Tap an item to open its energy line and buttons, tap again to close it; every item is closed again when the next wave starts. Items you can buy keep their Buy button in the row. The shop keeps your place when you buy something. The button before Start wave shows the enemies of the coming wave, one to a row with their picture and description, and the boss that follows a tenth wave.

## Energy

The Energy block at the top of the shop is the power of the whole tower. A new tower has none. Every weapon and extra draws energy while it is switched on: a base amount for having it plus a little for each upgrade level. The green background of an item shows its share of all the energy, and its energy line says how much it draws now. The switch on that line turns the module on or off, and the bar at the top changes at once.

Buy energy with coins during a run, or with points in the permanent shop; permanent energy and the on/off choices are what every new game starts with. There is no limit on how many weapons you own or run, only the energy. When the tower asks for more than it has, the bar turns red and the next wave cannot start until something is switched off or more energy is bought. In the permanent shop "Play again" waits in the same way.

## Weapons

The cannon, flamer, frost and laser fire at targets they choose themselves. Shots follow their target and, if it dies, go on to the nearest enemy. The mine layer drops mines at random places inside the range, one after another and without a limit on how many lie on the field; they go off when a ground enemy walks over them. The sweeping beam turns round the tower and cuts every enemy it passes inside the range, flyers too; Power makes each cut stronger and Spin speed makes it turn faster.

On phones the arena fills the portrait space with the tower in the centre. Landscape screens show a square arena. The simulation is the same in either view. The menu button holds Sound and Exit.

## Permanent progress and saves

Points earned from runs buy permanent upgrades for later games. The best wave is remembered. Use Exit to open Game Hub's save and exit prompt; a saved solo run can be continued later.

## Fast play

With the Premium build present and the owner's Fast play switch enabled, players can choose speeds from normal to ×10 and start waves automatically. Slower devices may run below the selected speed to keep the page responsive. Waves can run on by themselves up to a selected wave, up to the existing record; the shop then opens and speed returns to normal for that run.

The controls appear during waves. The wave list's first option, Intermediate stop, opens the shop after every wave; "After wave N" lets waves follow one another until that wave. A save icon appears only when you change the speed or the wave; press it to keep them for new games in this browser. Changing a running game does not replace the saved setup until Save is pressed again.

The owner's switch is in the desktop launcher. Without Premium it stays visible and opens the Premium dialog; public players simply have the ordinary game without Fast play controls.
