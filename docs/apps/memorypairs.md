# Remember the Pairs

Remember the Pairs is a memory game for one to six players. Open its launcher in mvmOS and choose Play to reach the public Game Hub page. Game Hub is required; the launcher offers to install it when it is missing. Players use their Apps Hub accounts and can join from computers, phones or tablets in a web browser.

## Set up a game

Choose one of nine picture themes: animals, food, nature, transport, games and music, faces, numbers, colours or shapes. Select a table from 6 to 48 pairs; the available sizes depend on the number of pictures in the theme. Choose a time limit per turn or play without a limit.

Tables up to 20 pairs use a flat grid. From 24 pairs, cards sit on a rotating 3D cylinder. Drag with a finger or mouse, or use the mouse wheel, to turn it; tap or click a card to reveal it. The whole cylinder fits vertically on the screen. If WebGL is unavailable, the game uses a flat grid instead.

## Find the pairs

Turn over two cards on your turn. A match removes the pair and gives you another turn. A mismatch stays visible briefly before the cards turn back and play passes to the next player. In a solo game you continue playing yourself.

Everyone sees each revealed card. Other players' cylinders turn to show it, but exploring the table before choosing a card stays private to each browser. The server keeps the unrevealed deck.

The player chips show whose turn it is and how many pairs each player found. The bar below shows the remaining turn time when a limit is enabled. The sound button mutes or restores game sounds on this device.

## Saves and results

Use Game Hub's Save & exit control to put down a solo game and continue it later. Multiplayer games cannot be saved by an individual player.

Completed games appear in Game Hub history and statistics. Points reflect pairs found per attempt, adjusted for the size of the table. Statistics include pairs found, accuracy, best streak, timeouts and the number of cards. Multiplayer results also contribute to Game Hub's ratings.
