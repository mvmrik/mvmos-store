# Jigsaw

Jigsaw is a picture puzzle played through Game Hub 1.19.1 or newer. Install both apps, open Jigsaw from the desktop and follow its Game Hub link, or find it in the Game Hub library. Sign in with an Apps Hub account to play. Friends can join the same room from computers, phones or tablets using a web browser.

## Choose a puzzle

Choose Nature, Animals or Travel, then browse the fifteen pictures or pick a surprise. The gallery marks pictures you have completed and shows your best time and points for each size. Choose Easy (24 pieces), Medium (54), Hard (96), Expert (150) or Master (216). A host chooses the picture and size for everyone in a multiplayer room.

## Build the picture

Drag pieces close to their places on the empty board to snap them in. Move around the larger table by dragging an empty area, and zoom with a pinch or mouse wheel. On a phone, loose pieces start in scrolling rows; on a computer, they start scattered around the board. **Tidy** switches their layout, **Flat** changes the table angle, and the box shows the finished picture. A hint places one piece for you and costs points.

There is no time limit. Placed pieces earn points, and finishing without hints adds a bonus. Finish within the time allowance for your chosen size to earn a bonus for each second left; taking longer never ends the game or subtracts points. Game Hub records your score, size, completed puzzles, pieces placed, time and hints.

In a solo game, **Save & exit** keeps the board and loose piece positions for later, including on another device. In a multiplayer game, everyone builds the same picture at their own pace. The highest score wins after all players finish. A player who finishes early can watch the others progress or leave; their result still counts.
