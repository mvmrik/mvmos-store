# Font licences

The bundled fonts come from the Google Fonts project (https://fonts.google.com) and are published by their designers under free licences that allow use, copying and redistribution:

- SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org): almost every family. A family keeps its own copyright notice, listed in its project at https://github.com/google/fonts.
- Apache License 2.0: Roboto Slab.
- Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0: Ubuntu and Ubuntu Mono.

The files are shortened to the Latin, Latin Extended and Cyrillic letters and saved as woff2 by the font service; the designs are unchanged. Fonts you upload yourself keep their own licence and are your responsibility.
